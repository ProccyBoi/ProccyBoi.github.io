What this model leaves out

Velocity saturationConstant mobility is assumed. Above roughly 104 V/cm of lateral field, drift velocity saturates and ID becomes closer to linear in overdrive than quadratic, so this model over-predicts.
Threshold derivationVth is a slider, not a computed quantity. The electrostatics belong to the assigned threshold-voltage page.
Body effectSource and body are assumed tied, so VSB = 0 and Vth does not shift.
Short-channel effectsNo threshold roll-off, DIBL or punch-through. The subthreshold tail here has no VDS dependence beyond the (1 − e−VDS/VT) factor.
Breakdown and reliabilityNo avalanche, punch-through or oxide breakdown at any bias.
TemperatureFixed at 300 K. kT/q = 25.85 mV throughout.
Values are illustrative; the model is for teaching, not design.
