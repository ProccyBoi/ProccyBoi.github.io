A useful cover

I sketch circuits on graph paper, then stop to check a package size or remember which symbol convention I meant. This notebook puts those references on the covers, where they cannot get buried under another browser tab.

Both covers are real fabricated boards. That makes the dimensions physical: a 0402 looks like a 0402, the trace-width scale can be felt and compared, and the whole thing is stiff enough to write on away from a desk.

What made the cut

The front ranges from SOT and SOIC families through QFN, LGA, BGA and larger leaded packages. It also includes common passive sizes and copper widths from 0.2 to 20 mm.

The other cover is a schematic-symbol sheet: passives, sources, diodes, transistors, switches, logic, amplifiers, antennas and transformers. I left the decorative traces visible rather than pretending the cover was a normal circuit board.
