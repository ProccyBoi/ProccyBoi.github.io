Overview

Developed live LoRa telemetry and flight-data systems, including avionics and ground-station PCBs, GNSS, IMU, environmental and strain sensing, BMS/data storage, and post-flight visualisation.

Andrew joined Mission Systems in August 2025 and became Avionics & Payload Team Lead in May 2026. He represented UNSW at AIAA DBF 2026 in Wichita.
