Accepted flight-log columns

Use the first worksheet and place field names in row 1. Column order does not matter; common Betaflight-style aliases are recognized.

Minimum viable replay

time_s or timestamp_ms, plus latitude and longitude. Altitude and attitude columns unlock the full 3D and instrument experience.
