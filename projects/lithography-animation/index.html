Clean, dehydrate, prime

A clean wafer surface is dehydrated and primed so photoresist wets uniformly and adheres during develop and etch.

Surface preparation controls adhesion

Water on oxide makes resist adhesion weak. HMDS treatment makes the surface more hydrophobic before coating.

Equations Used in the Animation

CD = k1 lambda / NA

Critical dimension improves with shorter wavelength and higher numerical aperture, but only when the process can support the required k1.

DOF = k2 lambda / NA^2

Higher NA improves resolution while reducing focus tolerance. A flatter wafer and tighter focus control become more important.

N_abs = eta D A / (hc/lambda); sigma_N/N ~= 1/sqrt(N_abs)

The photon estimate uses the selected square feature area and absorbed fraction. It shows Poisson scaling only, not a complete resist or defect model.
