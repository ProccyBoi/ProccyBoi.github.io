Radio and position

I designed this board as one half of a small telemetry link. An ESP32-WROOM-32E runs the application, a 915 MHz LoRa module handles the long-range connection, and a u-blox NEO-M9N supplies position data.

Putting the radio and GNSS on one board kept the wiring simple in use. It also made the RF layout impossible to ignore, which turned out to be the more valuable part of the project.

The first RF lesson

The Rev A feedline was not as close to 50 Ω as I wanted. I checked it with a VNA, took the mismatch seriously, and used what I found when I laid out the dedicated RF test board.

Even with that first-pass layout, the pair reached at least one kilometre in testing. That result was useful, but the measurement was better: it gave the next board a specific problem to fix.
