A city map is the surface. Beneath it: two copper layers, a hundred small decisions, and a physical connection to the world outside.

01 Route the invisible.

Every light.
A path back.

Front and back copper carry power and data through a map-shaped circuit. Pads, vias and routed tracks come directly from the production KiCad design.

The routing, solder-mask openings and silkscreen become a populated board. An ESP32 drives 116 RGB pixels across five electrical data chains.
