Tamagotchi SD Card assembly

Storage interface

The board pairs an STM32F072C8 with a full-size SD-card socket. USB-C and a seven-pin header provide the external connections, with reset and boot switches available on the PCB.

The two-layer board measures 44.88 × 39.27 mm.
