My work

I joined UNSW Skylabs in August 2025 and became Avionics & Payload Team Lead in May 2026. I represented UNSW at AIAA Design/Build/Fly 2026 in Wichita.

My work covers both ends of the link: the STM32 aircraft logger, the ESP32 LoRa ground station and the replay tools used after landing.

Open Flight Review ↗ Explore both boards View F110211 reference flight ↗

One data path, three views

The aircraft binary log is the high-rate record, scheduled at 40 Hz. A smaller telemetry packet crosses the 915 MHz link at up to 10 Hz, where the ground station records it again and forwards a live stream over USB or its own Wi-Fi dashboard.

After the flight, the same data contract feeds a replay tool with the 3D route, attitude, artificial horizon, altitude, speed and GPS readouts kept on one timeline. Treating the boards, packet format and analysis software as one system has made faults much easier to trace.
