Audio hardware

Kiku combines an STM32H523VET6 controller with a BM83 Bluetooth module, TLV320AIC3104 audio codec, MAX98357A amplifier and SI4705 FM receiver. A microSD socket provides removable storage.

An ST7789 display, rotary encoder and two buttons form the local controls. The board also includes a headphone jack.

Power and programming

A BQ25895 charger and MAX17048 fuel gauge handle the battery power circuit. The USB data connections feed the charger rather than the STM32; programming uses SWD.

The four-layer PCB has a nominal 60 × 105 mm outline, with component height and connector placement informing the surrounding enclosure.

Mechanical fit

The P2 enclosure follows a printed P1 revision. Its main body is approximately 77.1 × 120.9 mm with a nominal skin depth of 33.2 mm; the collapsed antenna and stand bring the overall envelope to approximately 79.1 × 126.7 × 56.1 mm.

Battery, display and antenna fit still require qualification for P2.
