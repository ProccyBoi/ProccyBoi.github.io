Dash

An ESP32 desk companion with an OLED display, touch input and a battery-powered enclosure.

Dash device assembly

The housing and PCB use the supplied design files. Display, speaker, battery, plugs, magnets and touch plate are dimensioned visualizations guided by build photos. Their placement illustrates the assembly; it does not verify manufacturing fit.

Inside the device

A 50 × 52 × 48 mm enclosure brings together the ESP32 board, a 128 × 64 OLED, speaker, capacitive touch plate and 1000 mAh battery. USB-C supplies charging and the wired programming interface.

Dash grew out of a vehicle-mounted sensor prototype. I adapted its hardware and rewrote the firmware for a desk companion, with a state machine coordinating the display, sensing, audio and focus sessions. A local Wi-Fi interface provides session control and settings.
