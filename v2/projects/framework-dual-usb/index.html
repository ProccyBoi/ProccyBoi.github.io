Why two?

A normal expansion card spends the whole bay on one connector. I wanted two. USB 2.0 was enough for the small peripherals I actually plug in, and keeping the scope there made the routing and power design realistic in the Framework card outline.

The finished board fits the standard clear shell, so it looks and behaves like the other modules instead of hanging off the laptop as an adapter.

The circuit

A CH334F handles the two-port hub. The TPS2065 switches and limits downstream power, and USBLC6-2SC6 parts sit close to the connectors for ESD protection. I also had to fit the crystal, configuration resistors and three Type-C connectors into a board that is mostly connector.

The layout is dense but still readable. The clear case makes that worthwhile: you can see the hub and power path when the card is installed.
