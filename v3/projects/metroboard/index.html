Sydney rail display

I wanted Sydney's rail network to live on the wall, not in another browser tab. Metroboard is a 300 × 305.7 mm PCB with 291 LEDs, laid out as a physical map of the network.

The board joins Wi-Fi and gets TfNSW GTFS real-time data through a service I wrote. Each update turns vehicle positions into light, so the useful part of the display is visible before I have even picked up my phone.

The PCB is the interface

There is no separate screen or printed overlay. The copper, solder mask, silkscreen and LEDs have to work together as both electronics and map graphics.

That constraint is what later led to TramTrace. The tram network needed a different scale and much tighter route geometry, but the idea stayed the same: make live transport data legible as a piece of hardware.
