Aircraft telemetry

Sensors and logging

I developed this PCB around an STM32G474RET6. It brings together a u-blox GNSS receiver, BNO08x IMU, BME280 barometer, digital and analogue airspeed inputs, an HX711 strain interface, RTC, microSD storage and an SX1262 radio.

Each sensor has its own useful rate, failure mode and definition of “fresh”. The firmware stores presence and validity beside each measurement; missing values are NaN, not zero.

Firmware and logging

The IMU supplies acceleration and gyro reports at 100 Hz, attitude at 50 Hz and magnetometer data at 25 Hz. GNSS runs at 10 Hz. The aircraft logger samples at a 40 Hz target, batches eight binary records into each SD transaction and syncs once a second, which avoids making every sample wait for a card write.

A compact SX1262 packet carries the live subset to the ground station. The default profile sends at 22 dBm every 100 ms using 500 kHz bandwidth and spreading factor 5. Ground commands can start or stop the aircraft log, add an event marker and change the link profile without a USB cable in the aircraft.

After landing

The binary record keeps GNSS, environment, attitude, airspeed, strain and estimator output together. My replay tool reads that file into a synchronized 3D path, aircraft attitude, artificial horizon and engineering plots, while leaving the recorded values untouched.
