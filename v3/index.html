A flight is more than
the aircraft.

My work connects the onboard logger, the radio receiver and the tools used after landing.

  1. Acquire & keep a record

    The STM32 board reads navigation and sensor data, then writes the aircraft’s binary log to microSD.

    Aircraft logger ↗

  2. Send a smaller picture

    A 915 MHz LoRa link carries telemetry to the ESP32 ground station, which keeps its own SD record.

    Ground station ↗

  3. Look back at what happened

    Flight Review puts attitude, route and sensor readings on one timeline. The F110211 reference includes the logging diagnostics, not just the flight path.

    Read the system story ↗

The logger’s 40 Hz rate is a design target. F110211 reports 50.0 ms log dt, 13,402 log overruns and 0 SD write errors.
