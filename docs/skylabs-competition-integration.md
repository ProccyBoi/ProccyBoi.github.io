This revision is prepared for review, not published. The trainer and competition taildragger enter together; opaque white/blue Solarfilm covers the competition structure, peels away, and reassembles on reverse scroll. The camera then reveals the internal installation. Only the sensor body drops and trails aft. The carriage, guides and housing remain stationary in the fuselage. ## Source identity and units

Source geometry is not resized. Runtime axis wrappers convert metres to nose +X, up +Y, span Z. `integration.json` records transforms and Library IDs. Build sources: `scripts/build-skylabs-competition.py`, `scripts/extract-skylabs-sensor.py`, `scripts/build-skylabs-film.py`.

The product `Sensor V2, 300mm Length` contains two solids, not just the sensor: solid 0 is the 300 × 63.8 × 63.8 mm body; solid 1 duplicates the separate `Cart V2` (identical 160278.1276 mm³ volume, with a 0.2093 mm X offset). The old animation moved that compound and therefore carried a duplicate cart. The revised runtime suppresses that compound and inserts an independently exported, unaltered solid 0. The original independent Cart V2 stays visible and fixed. `sensor-identification.json` records bounds and the extracted GLB hash. The previous payload reference and loose duplicate rudder are suppressed; the attached tail rudder remains.
