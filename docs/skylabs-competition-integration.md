# Competition aircraft — internal installation revision

This revision updates the published v2 sequence. The trainer and competition taildragger enter together; opaque white/blue Solarfilm covers the competition structure, peels away, and reassembles on reverse scroll. The camera then reveals the internal installation. The carriage lowers 25 mm inside the fuselage with the sensor, then the sensor alone releases vertically a further 450 mm. There is no aft translation. Guides and housing remain fixed; the carriage remains aboard. Trainer specifications and the same ESC remain provisional.
