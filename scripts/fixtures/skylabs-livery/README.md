# Trainer artwork sources

The existing `arc.png`, `unsw.png` and `skylabs.png` were supplied for the earlier wing livery. The trainer-only builder uses those images and these additional public sources:

- `ansys.svg` is the [Simple Icons Ansys silhouette](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/ansys.svg), distributed under [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md). `ansys.png` is a raster of that vector. The builder restores the amber A to the otherwise monochrome silhouette.
- `australia.svg` is the 2:1 [Australian flag vector](https://github.com/hampusborgos/country-flags/blob/main/svg/au.svg) from the country-flags collection, which records its [sources and license](https://github.com/hampusborgos/country-flags#credits). `australia.png` is its raster. It is proportioned to the requested 1.74:1 marking on the model.
- `leap.png` is the [LEAP sponsor artwork](https://github.com/Chen222Xi/UTAS-MotorSports/blob/9f6a302e3adb6e3cfb6f5e4f939f601121e32650/utas-motorsport/assets/images/leap.png) from a university motorsports [sponsors page](https://github.com/Chen222Xi/UTAS-MotorSports/blob/9f6a302e3adb6e3cfb6f5e4f939f601121e32650/utas-motorsport/page-sponsors.php).

The trademarks remain those of their respective owners. The Mad 4 Heli position is intentionally blank because a usable source was not available in this environment. The private reference photos are not used in these files.
