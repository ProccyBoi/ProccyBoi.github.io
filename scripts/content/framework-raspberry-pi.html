A closer look.

Turn the card, separate the layers, and follow the components that make it work.

A small board with a defined place.

The expansion-card format gives the design its boundaries: a compact outline, a straddle-mount USB-C plug and mounting points that line up with the reference enclosure. The board is 0.8 mm thick, leaving room for the components above it.

The RP2354B sits at the centre of the layout, with external flash, a crystal and the power circuit arranged around it. Two tactile controls sit at the outer end of the card.

Designed as an assembly.

Component height and connector position matter as much as the copper layout. The interactive view brings the board and reference housing together, then separates the parts to make the arrangement easier to read.

The straddle-mount connector sits at one end of the board, while the two tactile controls occupy the opposite edge.
