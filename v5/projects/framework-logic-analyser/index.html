01 / The work

Capture hardware with explicit safe states

PCB design + prototype firmware

My contribution

An STM32G474 card design with board-specific capture firmware and Python host tooling.

The task

Bring logic and serial-bus capture into a 26 × 30 mm expansion card while keeping passive monitoring from driving the bus under observation.

Design decisions

Timer-triggered DMA samples the GPIO inputs. Native USB CDC carries commands and captured bytes to a Python tool that exports VCD or CSV.

The firmware boots with the muxes disconnected, CAN in standby and the multiprotocol transceiver shut down. Route changes disable the drivers before reconnecting inputs.

Raw GPIO routes are limited to 3.3 V logic. RS-232 is excluded from passive capture because that transceiver mode also enables its transmitter.

02 / Evidence

What the work
shows.

What has been checked

Published host-side tests exercise binary framing, VCD/CSV export and command validation using simulated serial data. The hardware document maps firmware pins and safe states to the routed PCB.

Result and next step

The prototype firmware implements immediate capture with configured limits of 10 MS/s and 32,768 samples, plus passive Classical CAN monitoring. Hardware trigger and pre-trigger capture remain unimplemented.

Read the detailed project notes

Capture interfaces

The card brings logic and serial-bus capture into a Framework expansion bay. Its STM32G474 supports four-channel logic and SPI capture, two-channel UART and I²C capture, and passive monitoring of CAN, RS-485 and RS-422.

The firmware documents a maximum capture rate of 10 MS/s and a buffer of 32,768 samples. Those limits describe the acquisition modes; they do not imply that every protocol runs at the maximum rate.

Signal routing

Two MAX14778 multiplexers route the inputs. A TCAN3413 handles the CAN interface, while a THVD4411 provides the multiprotocol transceiver. A seven-pin connector brings the signals and power to the edge of the card.

The four-layer PCB measures 26 × 30 mm.
