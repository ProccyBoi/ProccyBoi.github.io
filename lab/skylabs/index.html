What the pair actually does

One flight,
two records.

  1. 01

    Sense

    GNSS, IMU, pressure, airspeed and strain inputs arrive at their own useful rates instead of being forced into one timing loop.

  2. 02

    Record

    The STM32 assembles a 40 Hz binary aircraft log while keeping freshness and validity beside every measurement.

  3. 03

    Transmit

    A compact live subset crosses the 915 MHz SX1262 link at up to 10 Hz, independently of the full onboard record.

  4. 04

    Receive

    The ESP32 validates, stores and presents each packet, while returning log, marker and radio-profile commands to the aircraft.

Firmware, made physical

The full-fidelity record stays on the aircraft. The smaller radio packet gives the field crew a live view and an independent ground log without allowing the LoRa schedule to dictate what the aircraft keeps.
