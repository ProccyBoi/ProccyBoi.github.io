Interactive board study / Sydney rail network

A map made
of hardware.

Metroboard V3 is a 300 × 305.7 mm PCB with 291 addressable LEDs. Metroboard turns the Sydney rail network into the PCB itself rather than putting the map on a separate display.

The surface keeps the physical route artwork while the controller edge is rebuilt in depth so the USB, ESP32 and supporting logic can be inspected as an assembly.
