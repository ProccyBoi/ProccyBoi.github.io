What one update actually does

A map is redrawn
24 bits at a time.

  1. 01

    Build the frame

    The ESP32 turns live vehicle positions into RGB values for the status pixel and four line-specific chains.

  2. 02

    Cross voltage domains

    The MC14504B shifts the controller's logic into the 5 V domain used by the LEDs.

  3. 03

    Consume and forward

    Each WS2812C keeps its first 24 bits and regenerates the rest for the next pixel. Position in the chain becomes the address.
