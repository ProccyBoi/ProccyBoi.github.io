On the board

The map is also
the circuit board.

Station names, route geometry and components all had to share a 207.81 × 94.55 mm board. I used the silkscreen as the map, with an ESP32 handling Wi-Fi and display logic.

A CH340C provides USB programming. Level shifting connects the controller to five LED data chains, carrying vehicle positions out across the network.
