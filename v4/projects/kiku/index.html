01My contribution

Circuit design, embedded firmware and enclosure development. Personal project, 2026.

02The constraint

Bring local files, FM radio and Bluetooth receiving into one tactile, handheld player.

03Design decisions

The STM32H523 brings three listening sources into a common 48 kHz PCM output domain for wired headphones and the built-in speaker. The BM83 receives Bluetooth audio from a phone; there is no transmit path to wireless headphones.

Audio DMA has a 2 ms service window. An 8,192-frame stereo FIFO provides roughly 171 ms of buffering, with local playback filled ahead of display work. The screen only sends the region that changed.

A push encoder and two keys control the interface, with ten selectable looks. The browser simulator runs the actual C screen renderer compiled to WebAssembly; its hardware events, library and radio data are simulated.

The nine-part P2 enclosure develops the printed P1 design with a larger battery bay, stronger port opening, removable display cradle and folding stand.

04What needed work

The display sits in a removable cradle behind a 1 mm clear lens. Its flex follows a U-fold studied at roughly 4.3 mm bend radius. Supplier contact dimensions and connector insertion geometry still need a real mating check.

05Evidence & validation

The 12 October 2026 firmware record includes a clean build, host tests, 130 renderer captures and flash readback verification. The renderer captures passed bounds and full-frame versus dirty-region equivalence checks.

06What this does not establish

These checks establish the tested firmware image and behaviour, not extended listening reliability or physical fit. Longer Bluetooth playback, SD insertion, output switching, power measurements and a P2 assembly test remain on the bench. The browser does not receive FM or pair over Bluetooth, and its demo music is separate from the STM32 audio pipeline.
