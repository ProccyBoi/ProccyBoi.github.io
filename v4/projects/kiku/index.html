Embedded systems

A pocket audio player for SD music, FM radio and Bluetooth receiving.

↓

Engineering record

Three sources. One audio system.

Working development hardware · P2 enclosure awaiting physical fit check

01My contribution

Circuit design, embedded firmware and enclosure development.

02The constraint

Bring local files, FM radio and Bluetooth receiving into one tactile, handheld player.

03Design decisions

The STM32H523 brings three listening sources into a common 48 kHz PCM output domain for headphones and the built-in speaker.

A push encoder and two keys control the interface. The screen updates only its changed region, while audio buffering is prioritised.

The nine-part P2 enclosure develops the printed P1 design with a larger battery bay, removable display cradle and folding stand.

04Evidence & validation

The browser simulator runs the actual C screen renderer compiled to WebAssembly. Hardware events, library data and radio data are simulated.

05What this does not establish

The P2 display flex and connector still need a physical mating check. The browser does not receive FM or pair over Bluetooth, and its demo music is separate from the STM32 audio pipeline.
