Assembled hardware

01My contribution

ESP32-S3 PCB, USB programming and power circuitry in the Framework expansion-card format.

02The constraint

Fit an ESP32 development board, its USB connection and the antenna keep-out into one Framework expansion bay.

03Design decisions

The 0.6 mm PCB supports direct USB-C mating without a separate interposer. The module's antenna end remains unobstructed in the clear enclosure.

A CH340K provides USB serial, a BC817 pair handles automatic boot and reset, and an AMS1117-3.3 supplies the module.

04Evidence & validation

The build photographs show the assembled black revision in its enclosure, close-ups of the programming and power circuit, and the earlier green PCB.

05Current outcome

The latest black two-layer board is assembled in the Framework shell, with the USB interface, power circuit and radio on one removable card.
