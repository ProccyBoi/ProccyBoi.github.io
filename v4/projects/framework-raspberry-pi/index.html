An RP2354B within the Framework outline

PCB design

01My contribution

A 26 × 30 mm RP2354B microcontroller-board design for a Framework laptop expansion bay.

02The constraint

Arrange the microcontroller, USB connector, power circuit and controls around the reference enclosure's mounting points.

03Design decisions

The 0.8 mm PCB uses a straddle-mount USB-C plug. Two tactile controls sit at the opposite edge from the connector.

The RP2354B occupies the centre of the layout, with flash, crystal and power circuitry arranged around it.

04Current outcome

The board layout and its relationship to the Framework reference enclosure are available to inspect in the CAD assembly.
