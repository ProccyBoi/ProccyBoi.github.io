Engineering record

From aircraft sensors to a flight record

01My contribution

I developed the STM32 aircraft logger, ESP32 LoRa ground station and post-flight replay tools. I joined Skylabs in August 2025 and became Avionics & Payload Team Lead in May 2026.

02The constraint

Sensors update at different rates, the radio carries a smaller live subset, and the team still needs a useful record after landing.

03Design decisions

Keep the aircraft binary log separate from the live link. Record the received packets again on the ground, then use one data contract for synchronized replay.

04Evidence & validation

The published F110211 flight contains 13,772 rows over 679.33 seconds. Its report records a 50.0 ms log interval, 13,402 log overruns and zero SD write errors.

05What this does not establish

40 Hz is the aircraft logger’s target. This flight does not demonstrate sustained 40 Hz logging; zero SD write errors does not mean every timing deadline was met.
